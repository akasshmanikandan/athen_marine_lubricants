Main Grades

Our Main Grades cover the core lubrication needs of marine diesel engines — including cylinder oils and system oils for slow-speed two-stroke engines and trunk piston engine oils (TPEO) for medium-speed engines. Formulated for thermal and oxidation stability, strong detergency/dispersancy, and compliance with MARPOL Annex VI sulphur limits and OEM specifications.

Applications

Main propulsion engines, diesel generator sets, two-stroke cylinder oils, medium-speed trunk piston engines.

Benefits & Compliance

Provides thermal stability and strong detergency. Fully compliant with MARPOL Annex VI, IMO Tier II, and OEM approvals.

Applications

Benefits & Compliance

Delivers consistent protection across auxiliary systems to reduce unplanned maintenance. Meets major gear & hydraulic OEM standards.

Benefits & Compliance

Engineered for extended drain intervals, higher thermal stability, and specialized OEM requirements beyond mineral oils.

EAL - Environmentally Acceptable Lubricants

Our EAL range is formulated for vessels operating under environmental discharge regulations such as the U.S. EPA Vessel General Permit (VGP), supporting stern tubes, thrusters, and other oil-to-sea interfaces.

Applications

Stern tube seals, thrusters, controllable pitch propellers, and oil-to-sea interface equipment.

Benefits & Compliance

Formulated for vessels under discharge regulations. Fully compliant with U.S. EPA Vessel General Permit (VGP) standards.
