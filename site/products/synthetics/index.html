Synthetics

Our synthetic range is engineered for demanding operating conditions where extended drain intervals, higher thermal stability, or specialized OEM requirements call for more than a conventional mineral-based oil.

Applications

Turbines, compressors, high-load, and high-temperature systems requiring premium oils.

Benefits & Compliance

Engineered for extended drain intervals, higher thermal stability, and specialized OEM requirements beyond mineral oils.
