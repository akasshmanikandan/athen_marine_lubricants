Secondary Grades

OEM-approved marine lubrication solutions. Intellectual Engineering. Delivering Trust.

Secondary Grades

Secondary Grades support auxiliary systems and equipment that fall outside main engine lubrication — including gear oils, hydraulic oils, and system oils formulated for marine-specific operating conditions.

Applications

Gearboxes, hydraulic systems, and auxiliary machinery operating under marine conditions.

Benefits & Compliance

Delivers consistent protection across auxiliary systems to reduce unplanned maintenance. Meets major gear & hydraulic OEM standards.
