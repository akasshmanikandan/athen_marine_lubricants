## Our Products

###  Main Lubricants

 Quality lubricants are designed for modern marine engines including those for piston cooling, power generation and other industrial applications.

###  Secondary Lubricants

 High-performance marine lubricants designed for diesel engines, chain drives, slide guides, plain and rolling gears, marine hydraulic systems, compressors and turbines.

###  Synthetic Lubricants

 Advanced fully synthetic lubricants developed for use in rotary, reciprocating or centrifugal compressors, gears, bearings, refrigeration and air conditioning systems.

###  Greases

 Heavy-duty calcium sulfonate complex soap-based greases for use in a wide range of marine, industrial and automotive applications.
