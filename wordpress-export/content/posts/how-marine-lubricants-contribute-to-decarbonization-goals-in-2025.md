### The Push for Cleaner Shipping

Regulatory bodies and shipping customers alike are demanding cleaner operations. Environmental standards are tightening worldwide, and carbon taxes, port emissions requirements, and green shipping incentives are already reshaping how fleets operate.

To stay competitive and compliant, operators must optimize every aspect of performance — including how engines are lubricated.

### Lubricants as Efficiency Enablers

Marine lubricants directly affect engine performance and fuel efficiency. High-quality lubricants reduce internal friction, prevent deposit formation, and improve thermal stability. As a result, engines run cleaner, smoother, and more efficiently — consuming less fuel per nautical mile.

This is not only good for operational budgets but also contributes directly to a vessel’s CO₂ emissions profile.
