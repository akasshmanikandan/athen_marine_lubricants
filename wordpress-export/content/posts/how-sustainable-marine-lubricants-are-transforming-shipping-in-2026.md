Sustainability is now at the heart of global maritime operations. The industry has entered a new era in which environmental performance is directly tied to competitive advantage. In 2026, sustainable marine lubricants are playing a critical role in helping fleets lower emissions, reduce waste, and meet evolving global regulations.

### **Why Sustainability Matters More Than Ever**

Shipping companies face increasing regulations and expectations:

- IMO’s strengthened 2026 GHG and pollution standards
- Stricter monitoring of waste oil disposal
- Rising demand for greener supply chain partners
- Carbon reporting requirements for international shipping
Lubricants have become a core part of sustainability strategies because they directly influence emissions, fuel efficiency, and engine cleanliness.

### **How Modern Sustainable Lubricants Reduce Environmental Impact**

New-generation eco-conscious lubricants deliver environmental benefits without sacrificing performance:

#### **1. Reduced Emissions Through Improved Engine Efficiency**

By lowering friction and keeping the engine cleaner, sustainable lubricants reduce CO₂ output per voyage.

#### **2. Lower Oil Consumption**

High-performance, oxidation-resistant formulations require fewer top-ups and longer drain intervals — reducing total lubricant waste.

#### **3. Biodegradable & Eco-friendly Options**

For environmentally sensitive regions, biodegradable lubricants minimize ecological impact in case of leaks or spills.

#### **4. Cleaner Engines, Cleaner Exhaust**

Sustainable lubricants reduce deposits, resulting in smoother combustion and lower particulate emissions.
