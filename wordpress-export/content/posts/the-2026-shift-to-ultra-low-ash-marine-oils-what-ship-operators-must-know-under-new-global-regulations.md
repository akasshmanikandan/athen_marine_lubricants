Starting in 2026, new global regulations make ultra-low ash marine oils essential for modern vessels. - **Why Ultra-Low Ash Oils Are Critical in 2026:**
Ash deposits in modern engines increase wear, reduce efficiency, and elevate emissions. Ultra-low ash oils minimize these deposits, promoting cleaner combustion and longer engine life.
- **Technical Benefits:**

- **Reduced Engine Deposits:** Improves heat transfer and lowers wear.
- **Extended Maintenance Intervals:** Fewer oil changes and reduced downtime.
- **Emission Compliance:** Meets new IMO and EU regulations for 2026.

- **Advantages for Modern Fleets:**
Shipping operators gain stable engine performance, lower fuel consumption, and reduced operational costs. UL-Ash oils are especially beneficial for dual-fuel and LNG engines, which are sensitive to deposits.
