In the highly competitive world of maritime operations, every dollar counts. While low-cost marine lubricants may seem like a smart way to reduce operational expenses, the reality is often the opposite. Over time, the hidden costs of using subpar lubricants can significantly outweigh the initial savings — leading to higher maintenance costs, increased downtime, and even damage to critical engine components.

### Short-Term Savings, Long-Term Losses

Cheaper marine lubricants are often produced with lower-quality base oils and additives. While they may meet the minimum requirements for short-term operation, they usually fail to provide consistent protection under high load, extreme temperatures, or extended usage. This leads to faster wear and tear, sludge buildup, and corrosion inside engines and auxiliary machinery.

A common consequence? Unscheduled maintenance and unexpected repair costs — not to mention the potential interruption of operations at sea, where downtime can cost thousands of dollars per hour.

### The Price of Downtime

Marine operators know that even minor technical issues at sea can escalate quickly. Poor lubrication can cause overheating, friction damage, or bearing failure — resulting in a vessel being taken offline for inspection or repair. The cost of towing, emergency parts, and labor quickly adds up.

Beyond financial loss, downtime can jeopardize delivery schedules, contract obligations, and your company’s reputation with clients and partners.
