##### As winter approaches across global shipping routes, vessel operators face a familiar challenge: protecting engines, auxiliary systems, and hydraulic components from the harsh effects of low temperatures. For the maritime industry, where operational efficiency directly impacts commercial performance, ensuring reliable engine lubrication is no longer optional — it is a mission-critical requirement.

In 2025, shipping companies are under increasing pressure to maintain uptime, reduce emissions, and optimize costs. High-performance marine lubricants, specifically formulated for winter conditions, play a key role in achieving these goals.

### **The Winter Challenge: Low Temperatures and Engine Stress**

Cold weather affects marine engines in several ways:

- **Increased oil viscosity**, leading to slower circulation during startup
- **Higher friction and wear**, especially in cylinder liners and bearings
- **Sluggish fuel combustion**, putting additional stress on piston rings
- **Moisture accumulation**, accelerating corrosion and sludge formation
For vessels operating in colder regions — including Europe, North America, and high-altitude ports — the risk of mechanical failure rises significantly during winter months.
